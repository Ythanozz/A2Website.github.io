What is MONSTER HUNTER: WILDS?

Wilds is an action role-playing video game played from a third-person perspective. In Wilds, players take on the role of a custom hunter character who travels to the "Forbidden Lands", a landmass filled with monsters, in order to locate a missing expedition party. As with previous games in the series, the player's hunter tracks and fights monsters, either capturing or slaying them, from which they gain rewards in the form of monster parts and other resources. Along with other resources collected in the game world, the player can then craft new armor, weapons, consumables, and other gear, which allow them to fight more difficult monsters.

Up to 14 Classes to choose!
